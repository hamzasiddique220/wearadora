# How to Choose Comfortable Heels — A Guide by Karachi Shoemakers

Heels have a reputation for looking beautiful and feeling painful — but that trade-off is mostly about choosing the wrong pair, not about heels themselves. After more than 40 years of making footwear by hand in Karachi, here is what we tell customers who want heels they can wear to a full event without counting the minutes until they can take them off.

## 1. Match the heel height to how long you'll wear them

Heel height is the single biggest comfort factor.

- **Kitten heels (about 1–2 inches):** the most comfortable option for long days, walking and standing. Elegant without straining the foot.
- **Mid heels (about 2–3 inches):** a good balance of height and comfort for weddings and dinners.
- **High heels (3 inches and up):** best kept for shorter, seated occasions.

If you're unsure, start lower. A [kitten heel](/product/knot-detail-kitten-heel) gives you the flattering line of a heel with far less pressure on the ball of the foot.

## 2. Choose a block heel over a thin heel for stability

A wider **block heel** spreads your weight across a larger base, so you feel steady instead of balancing on a point. Styles like our [block heel embellished slides](/product/beige-block-heel-embellished-slides) are much easier to walk in on uneven floors, lawns and mehndi setups than a stiletto — while still looking dressy.

## 3. Look for a supportive strap

A strap across the top of the foot or around the ankle holds your foot in place, so your toes aren't sliding forward into the front of the shoe. Multi-strap designs such as our [multi-strap heels](/product/elegant-black-multi-strap-heels) keep the foot secure and reduce the rubbing that causes blisters.

## 4. Get the fit right

- Shop for shoes later in the day, when feet are slightly larger.
- There should be a small gap at the back — your heel shouldn't slip out or be jammed in.
- Most of our designs run in sizes 6 to 11; check the size list on each product page and message us on WhatsApp if you're between sizes.

## 5. Prioritise real materials and finishing

Comfort also comes from how a shoe is built — a smooth lining, a padded footbed and clean stitching stop the small irritations that turn into pain by the end of the night. Because every pair is handcrafted in-house, we finish the inside of the shoe as carefully as the outside.
